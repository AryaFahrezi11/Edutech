Privacy Policy

Welcome to Edutech!

Last Updated: July 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Edutech! We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users, especially children. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Edutech Android application.

Information Collected

We strive to collect only the minimum amount of information necessary to provide a safe, engaging, and personalized educational experience:

  • Account Information: We collect your email address and password for account registration and authentication purposes.
  • Learning & Progress Data: We may collect data regarding your learning progress and leaderboard statistics to enhance your educational experience and provide in-app features.
  • Device Information: We may collect non-identifiable technical data (such as OS version or app diagnostics) to ensure the app runs smoothly and to fix bugs.

Account Registration (Email & Password)

To use Edutech, users must register an account using a valid email address and password. We employ a One-Time Password (OTP) system sent to your email to verify your account during registration.

Google Gemini AI

Edutech integrates with Google Gemini AI to provide interactive, smart, and adaptive learning experiences. User queries and educational prompts made within the app may be processed by Google's Gemini AI services to generate helpful responses. Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information in their prompts.

Camera Permission (Object Hunt)

Our "Object Hunt" educational feature requires access to your device's camera. The camera is used solely in real-time to recognize educational objects and complete interactive tasks. We do not record, store, or transmit any images or video data to our servers. All object recognition processing is transient.

Children's Privacy

Edutech is carefully designed with children in mind. We comply with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent, and our application does not contain behavioral or personalized advertising.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access to personal information is strictly limited.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share data only under the following strictly limited circumstances:

  • With trusted service providers (e.g., our email delivery service for OTPs) who are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • When explicitly required by law or to protect the rights, property, and safety of our users.

User Rights

You retain full control over your data. You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of any inaccurate data.
  • Request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

Privacy Policy Updates

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or relevant laws. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top. We encourage parents and guardians to review this policy periodically.
