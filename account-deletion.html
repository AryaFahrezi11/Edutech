Account Deletion Request

Last Updated: July 2026

How to Request Deletion

At Edutech, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and personal data. To request the deletion of your account, please send an email to:

aplikasiedutech@gmail.com

Please ensure your email includes:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Your Registered Email: The email address associated with your Edutech account.

Data to be Deleted

Upon processing your request, the following data will be permanently deleted from our systems:

  • Account information
  • Learning progress
  • Leaderboard data
  • Parent account information

Data Retention

Please note that some information may be retained securely if strictly required by law or necessary for legal compliance.

Processing Time

We aim to process all valid account deletion requests within 30 days of receiving them.
